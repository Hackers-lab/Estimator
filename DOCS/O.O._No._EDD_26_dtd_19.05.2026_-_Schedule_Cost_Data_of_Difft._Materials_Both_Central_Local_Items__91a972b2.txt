Subject: Updating prices of different materials (Central & Local Purchase)
items for the Financial Year 2026-2027 in respect of 33 KV, 11 KV & LT
System.
Presently the estimation of different schemes are being done on the basis of prices of
different materials (Central & Local Purchase) items as per standard schedule published
in the FY 2023- 2024 for 33 KV, 11 KV & LT system.
The methodology of updating price has been adopted in line with the guidelines framed
by the Corporate Finance Department, WBSEDCL. The updated prices of all Central
Purchase items have been confirmed by the Chief Engineer (P & C) WBSEDCL. The
prices of Local Purchase items have been updated by considering the average of the
available prices for the period of 01.07.2023 to 12.03.2026 as received from the ERP
system.
In order to facilitate the process of issuing (T & A) approval for different schemes in ERP
system, the material code of each item along with the material description as per the
ERP system has been specifically indicated.
The prices as shown against each item are only for estimation purpose and should not
be considered as reference for purchase of the same. This practice is to be followed with
immediate effect.
